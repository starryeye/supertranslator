## Review Focus

1. A diagonal/curved/broken outline can have the same bounding box as a rectangle; only real complete frame geometry is evidence. Task 2: `test_composite_rejects_inferred_or_broken_frame`.
2. Wrapped captions and intervening ordinary prose must not become artwork or allow captions to reach through body text. Task 2: `test_composite_caption_and_body_have_exact_disjoint_targets` and `test_composite_refuses_intervening_body`.
3. Nearby figures/captions may have ambiguous or cross-page associations; never pick the first candidate. Tasks 1/2: `test_caption_match_rejects_ambiguity_and_orphans` and `test_composite_requires_unique_caption_relation`.
4. Scale/origin/frame-encoding differences and edge-crossing glyphs must not change source ownership silently. Task 2: `test_native_composite_ownership_across_encodings` and `test_composite_refuses_partial_glyph`.
5. Newly accepted extraction can still have missing, stale or explicitly failed AI approval. Task 3: `test_composite_publication_requires_exact_current_approval` across assembly/prepare/finalize.
